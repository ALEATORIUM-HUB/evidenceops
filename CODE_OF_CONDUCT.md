# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
use constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on ideas and behavior.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of another
person's private information are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to project discussions, contributions, and representation
of the project. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an existing
trusted channel. Maintainers will review reports discreetly and fairly, and may warn,
limit participation, or remove participants based on severity and repeated behavior.

This is the project's own short conduct policy, not a claim of adopting a particular
external covenant verbatim.
